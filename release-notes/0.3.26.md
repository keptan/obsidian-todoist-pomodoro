## Task dates and time totals

- Show the closest assigned date or deadline on tasks, parents, and projects, including dates on collapsed subtasks. Deadlines take precedence over assigned dates.
- Use theme colors for due-date bubbles, with ❗ for today and negative day counts for overdue dates.
- Show matching bubbles for active subtask counts and total tracked time, including completed subtasks. Recover parent/project links from completed-task history so older sessions contribute to totals.
- Keep nested subtasks in their project even when Todoist returns children before parents.

## Activity selection and session planning

- Suggest active Todoist tasks and custom activities together in Track activity and Log time, ordered by most recently tracked. Search by activity name or project.
- Log time directly against a selected Todoist task.
- Choose a session-specific work/rest plan before starting an activity or selected task. Stretch the outer handle to set 5–120 minutes on a nonlinear scale, and move the inner divider to adjust the split.
- Show work/rest minutes beside the slider, using the theme's green and red. Support keyboard and touch input.
- Start each plan from configured defaults without changing saved settings. Remind at the selected work target and calculate breaks using the selected ratio.

Minimum Obsidian version: 1.13.0.
